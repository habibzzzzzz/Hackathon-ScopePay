# Anti Slop audit 001

Date: 8 October 2026 (Asia/Jakarta). Mode: **After**, selected by the user. Scope: implemented demo workspace and public approval UI. This is an audit report, not a production-release approval. No finding below has been fixed as part of the audit.

Source direction: `docs/design (1).md`. Dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. The requested dark financial workspace, Poppins typography, restrained glass surface and baseline/request/charge composition are present.

## Findings awaiting selection

1. **HIGH, R-03 (mobile target size).** Some links have touch targets below the 44 × 44 px requirement in the mobile skill and supplied design. Measurements at 375 px: Workflow/Pricing/Terms/Privacy are about 20 px high; Create manually and All projects are about 23 px; project-name links are about 20 px; the wordmark is about 40 px. Source: `src/app/globals.css`, marketing navigation/footer and content links. Proposed fix: give standalone links and the wordmark a 44 px hit area while preserving inline prose links and spacing. Recheck dashboard, project detail and marketing at 375 px.
2. **HIGH, R-25 / antislop-human (non-text contrast).** Idle input boundaries use `#425169` over `#08111F`, a measured ratio of **2.35:1**, below the 3:1 boundary requirement. Against the solid panel `#0D1828`, the ratio is **2.22:1**; glass surfaces require a separate composited check. Secondary control borders also need review (`#2A3A50` against the panel: **1.54:1**). Source: input/select/textarea and secondary button styles in `src/app/globals.css`. Proposed fix: adjust control-boundary colors and verify 3:1 against both input interiors and surrounding surfaces. Keep the current focus accent, which measures **8.49:1** against the solid panel.

Approve specific numbers before audit-driven edits. A clean implementation/build result does not waive these UI findings.
